# HoopLoopSim Alpha 0.9 — Test Checklist

## Rating model
- [ ] Verify page reports Alpha 0.9 and +10 displayed OVR.
- [ ] A player whose 15 attributes average 80 displays as 90 OVR.
- [ ] An Alpha 0.8 save keeps approximately/exactly the same displayed OVR after migration.
- [ ] Naturally generated 90 OVR players usually do not begin with multiple 99 attributes.

## Progression
- [ ] Sim several young-player offseasons and confirm the direction/size is not identical each year.
- [ ] Confirm young players can occasionally stagnate or regress.
- [ ] Confirm rare big early jumps still exist.
- [ ] Confirm positive growth becomes noticeably harder above 90/95/98 in an individual attribute.
- [ ] Confirm veteran decline is still normal but not universal every offseason.

## Scoring balance
- [ ] Sample at least 20–30 games.
- [ ] Typical team scores should cluster much more often around 115–140 than 140–150.
- [ ] Sub-100 team scores should occasionally occur.
- [ ] 150+ and 160+ scores remain possible but are less routine.
- [ ] Very high shootouts remain possible as outliers rather than normal results.
- [ ] Box-score player points still add up to the team score.

## Regression checks
- [ ] Creator, draft, regular season, playoffs, offseason, saves and multi-season careers still load.
- [ ] International tournament still reaches Bronze then Gold and records medal history.
- [ ] League History and Career tabs still render.
