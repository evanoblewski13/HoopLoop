# HoopSim Alpha 0.2 test checklist

## Creator

- Type `3` for season length: it becomes 14.
- Type `120`: it becomes 99.
- Create a player below 50 OVR and confirm the draft button remains available.
- Create an undrafted-level player and confirm only three offers appear.

## Season

- Simulate games and confirm DNP entries say either `Injury` or `Coach's decision`.
- Open Roster and switch between every league team.
- Compare a losing team's young-player minutes with a winning team's rotation.

## Awards and playoffs

- Confirm every All-HoopLoop team displays PG, SG, SF, PF, and C.
- Confirm names, positions, team abbreviations, and overalls are visible.
- Sim a playoff round and confirm the page stays near the postseason panel.
- Confirm the bracket advances and every series ends at the correct wins-needed total.

## Multi-season career

- Continue after the first offseason into Season 2.
- Finish four drafted seasons and confirm exactly three free-agency offers appear.
- Sign with a team and confirm the next season begins there.
- Check that generated rosters remain at 12 players and no team exceeds two simultaneous injuries.
- Continue long enough to observe aging, development variance, generated retirements, and new young players.

## Retirement

- Retire manually during an offseason.
- Confirm the final card shows seasons, games, totals, averages, accolades, and Hall probability.
- For a career above 80%, confirm a second HoopLoop Hall of Fame screen appears.
- Save, reload, export, and import the completed career.
