# HoopSim Alpha 0.3 design notes

## Easter-egg names

The following names are eligible to appear occasionally in generated leagues or later rookie classes. A career usually contains none or one; they are intentionally not guaranteed:

Parker Fontaine, Bryant Wright, Bryan Stender, Beau Lasky, Dominic Kane, Peyton Kane, James Gross, Michael Tushar, Evan Oblewski, Adam Kane, Will Pollack, Josh Pritzl, Gavin Stelter, Jack Seibs, Brian Huo, Michael Tang, and Benjamin Fisher.

## Manual draft

The user remains a player rather than a general manager. Manual Draft advances the CPU-controlled draft one pick at a time and reveals the selected prospect's profile. It does not let the career player choose another team's selection.

## Hall of Fame visibility

The ongoing probability is hidden until the user is age 30 or has completed ten seasons, whichever occurs first. Retirement always reveals the final probability.
