# HoopSim Alpha 0.4 Test Checklist

## Existing save

- [ ] Load an Alpha 0.3 save.
- [ ] Confirm the career opens without losing history.
- [ ] Confirm the user has a jersey number.
- [ ] Confirm saved playoff seeds remain visible when data exists.

## Creator and mobile season input

- [ ] Set a jersey number, including `0` or `00`.
- [ ] Type a season length below 14 and leave the field; it becomes 14.
- [ ] Type or paste a season length above 99; it becomes 99.
- [ ] Confirm minus and plus buttons remain visible on mobile.

## Statistics model

- [ ] Create a very tall center with elite rebounding and vertical.
- [ ] Confirm size and ratings noticeably affect rebounds.
- [ ] Create an elite playmaker and compare assists on strong and weak teams over multiple seasons.
- [ ] Confirm ordinary players still produce believable totals.

## Awards and trophy case

- [ ] Finish a regular season.
- [ ] Confirm scoring, rebounding, assists, steals, and blocks leaders are displayed.
- [ ] Lead a category and confirm the matching trophy is added.
- [ ] Confirm repeated leader awards show a count in the trophy case.

## Playoffs

- [ ] Begin the playoffs and confirm seeds are shown.
- [ ] Advance to later rounds and confirm the original seeds remain beside teams.
- [ ] Confirm later rounds are vertically centered relative to the first round.
- [ ] Use **Sim one game per series** and confirm every unfinished series advances once.
- [ ] Confirm no new round begins until every current series is complete.
- [ ] Confirm starter minutes rise in playoff box scores.
- [ ] Open multiple game chips and inspect full box scores.
- [ ] Confirm user playoff games appear in Recent Games and the postseason log.
- [ ] Confirm the viewport stays near the playoff controls while simulating.

## League News

- [ ] Complete an offseason and open News.
- [ ] Confirm signings appear when generated contracts expire.
- [ ] Confirm retirements appear when players retire.
- [ ] Complete an approved trade request and confirm it appears under Trades.

## Free agency and team changes

- [ ] Reach free agency below 70 OVR and confirm three choices.
- [ ] Test one or more higher overall milestones.
- [ ] Sign with a new team.
- [ ] Open Roster and confirm the selector automatically shows the user's new team.

## Trade request

- [ ] Confirm the button only appears as usable in the offseason while under contract.
- [ ] Confirm it can only be attempted once per offseason.
- [ ] Test a denied request.
- [ ] Test an approved request and confirm the user changes teams.
- [ ] Confirm both rosters retain twelve players.

## Retirement and legacy

- [ ] Retire a career and inspect shooting percentages, teams, accolades, and Hall probability.
- [ ] Confirm qualifying jersey-retirement teams appear.
- [ ] Confirm the Hall of Fame screen still appears at 80% or higher.
