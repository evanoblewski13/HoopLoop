# Stats Race v4 data notes

- Current career-stat snapshot: through the completed 2025-26 regular season.
- SPG/BPG/TPG/FG%: regular-season career values, displayed to one decimal.
- Early-era STL/BLK/TOV: omitted where the NBA did not officially track the statistic across the player's career; never forced to zero.
- Triple-doubles: regular season only. Audited values are included; unaudited values are omitted until verified.
- Career earnings: NBA player salary only through 2025-26, shown in millions. Endorsements, net worth, future salary and other income are excluded.
- Team history: means the player actually appeared in an NBA game for the franchise.
- Salary source used for audited current profiles: Spotrac career-earnings pages.
- Career-stat cross-checks: StatMuse / Basketball Reference-style career totals and per-game rows.
