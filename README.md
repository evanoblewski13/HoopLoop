# HoopLoopSim Alpha 0.8

Alpha 0.8 focuses on career memory, international-tournament presentation, player-generation variety, and early-career development.

## Highlights
- Keeps the Alpha 0.7 +5 displayed OVR model.
- Renames the old History tab to **Career** and adds a separate **League History** tab.
- Career history now records International Basketball Tournament seed, group record, finish, medal, and tournament stats.
- League History records season champions, Finals MVPs, MVP, ROTY, DPOY, 6MOTY, COTY, and international medalists.
- International Bronze and Gold medal games are separate rounds. Bronze is played first by the two semifinal losers.
- Adds a **Sim 1 game** control to the international knockout stage.
- Adds a medal-podium presentation after the Gold Medal Game.
- Slightly increases hidden easter-egg-name frequency.
- Adds additional international first and last names while preserving the existing generated-name pool.
- Adds rare generated height outliers above 7 feet and below 6 feet.
- Gives first-year starters and young players a higher—but still non-guaranteed—chance of major offseason improvement.

No Supabase or SQL changes are required.
