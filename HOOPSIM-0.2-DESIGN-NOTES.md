# HoopSim Alpha 0.2 design notes

## Implemented feedback

- Games-per-season input is clamped to 14–99 for arrows and manual typing.
- The 50 OVR creation minimum is removed.
- Undrafted players receive three offers.
- Game logs distinguish injury DNPs from coach-decision DNPs.
- Rotation logic gives young players more opportunity on losing teams without forcing winning teams to bench stronger veterans.
- Every team roster is viewable.
- All-HoopLoop and All-Defensive teams display position, team, and overall; each group is position-balanced.
- Playoffs use a horizontal bracket presentation and remain anchored near the postseason area after simulation.
- Development has higher variance for players under 23, normal aging curves, rare adult breakouts, rare elite longevity, and injury-driven early retirement possibilities.
- Complete multi-season careers, contract expiration, three-team free agency, generated player movement, new rookies, retirement, career totals, Hall of Fame probability, and an automatic Hall induction screen above 80% are included.

## Still simplified

- Trades are not active yet.
- Generated rookies fill roster openings rather than running through a visible offseason draft.
- Coaching systems remain rating-based.
- Public career cards remain a future online option.
- Hall of Fame probability is a transparent game formula, not a real-world model.
